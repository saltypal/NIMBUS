# Shift handover

An unresolved fault stays visible across shifts. Record the observation, what was checked, the check result, any corrective action, and the next responsible role.

## Inspection and closure

“Checked” means that a check was mentioned. It does not mean the issue was fixed. Acknowledging a handover records that a person saw the current information; it does not close the fault.

## Verification

Only a confirmed operator verification satisfying the approved policy can authorize automatic closure. Missing evidence remains unknown. Never invent a passing result.
